Conflict Handling Rules

To protect farmers and cooperative groups from accidental price overwrites, the application follows a strict resolution protocol:

1. Price Locks (किंमत निश्चिती):

Remote server accepts the earliest finalized acceptance time stamp. Outdated counter-proposals are marked for manual re-confirmation.

2. Aggregate Pool Weights (एकत्रित वजन):

Incremental additive merge applies to weight dispatch. Reductions require two-party biometric verification.

3. Profile & Contact Records (शेतकरी माहिती):

Latest local timestamp overrides static contact cards unless locked by state registrar auditing.
